Geometry
Arc construction. A circle with only its right and top borders colored, rotated 45°, so the stroke spans 180° opening to the left. Pink arc stroke is ⅛ of its diameter (42px arc → 6px stroke at logo size; 30px → 5px at 88px icon; 19px → 3px at 32px).
Trace. A 1.5px chartreuse arc, same construction, 18px larger in diameter than the pink arc, concentric.
Source. A pink disc at the centre, ⅓ the pink arc's diameter, counter-rotated −45° so nothing inherits the tilt.
Rotation caution. Rotating 45° makes the element's bounding box 1.41× its width — inside a fixed tile, keep the arc's total size (diameter + both strokes) at or under 70% of the tile, or the ink escapes the corners.
Period. Diameter ≈ 28% of the wordmark's cap height, sitting on the baseline, gap of 3–5px after the T.
Live tag. Fill #C2447F, white 6px dot, white Space Mono 9–10px/700 at 0.14em, radius 4px, padding 4–5px × 9–10px. The deepened pink is what makes white type legible; palette pink #E97BB2 measures about 2.3:1 against white.
